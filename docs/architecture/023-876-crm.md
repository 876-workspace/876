# 023 — 876 CRM

**Status:** accepted and implemented.

876 CRM is the customer operations and relationship management service for the 876 ecosystem: an organization tracks **customers**, handles customer **requests**, routes work to **teams**, builds dynamic **forms**, and coordinates follow-ups with **notes**, **emails**, **tasks**, **reminders**, and **calendar events**. It runs as a standalone application on port 3007 and is administered from Console, both over the same data service on port 4010.

## Placement & Boundaries

CRM is a bounded context with its own datastore, not part of the identity core. It references the 876 organization as an opaque `organization_id` with **no cross-database foreign keys**, resolving names, memberships, and entitlements through the Workspace and Platform clients.

| Concern                                       | Owner                                     |
| --------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| Organization identity and member accounts     | `apps/api` (identity core)                |
| App launch entitlement (`876-crm`)            | `subscriptions` in the identity core      |
| Financial customer profiles, ledger, invoices | `apps/billing-api` (financial data plane) |
| Tasks, reminders, scheduling events           | `apps/work-api` (productivity plane)      |
| Requests, notes, teams, priorities, forms     | `apps/crm-api`                            |
| Operator administration across organizations  | `apps/console`                            |

## The Request Model: Beyond Strict Sales Funnels

Traditional CRMs (such as Zoho Bigin) center entirely around a "Deal" progressing through fixed sales pipeline stages. 876 CRM replaces this with **Requests**:

- A single record type that handles sales inquiries, customer onboarding, ongoing service requests, and account operations.
- Sequential numbering per tenant (`#1`, `#2`, `#3`...) allocated atomically from `next_request_number`.
- Rich taxonomy: Status (`OPEN`, `IN_PROGRESS`, `WAITING`, `RESOLVED`, `CLOSED`, `CANCELLED`), priority, category, subcategory, team, assignee, and requester contact.
- Inbound channels: `FORM`, `WIDGET`, `CHAT`, `EMAIL`, `API`, `AGENT`.

## Financial Customer Separation

CRM deliberately **never stores or duplicates financial customer records**.

- `crm_customer_profiles` stores only CRM-local state (`status`, `ownerId`) and an opaque `billing_customer_id`.
- The CRM application fetches customer identity, billing address, accounts receivable, and invoices server-to-server via `@876/billing/integration`.
- This eliminates the chronic synchronization lag, duplicate contact records, and conflicting billing data common in traditional CRM/accounting integrations.

## Productivity Plane Delegation (876 Work)

CRM previously accumulated internal `RequestTask` and `RequestReminder` models. Under ADR 019 (`docs/architecture/019-work-service-and-productivity-plane.md`), tasks, reminders, and scheduling events belong to **876 Work** (`apps/work-api`):

- CRM requests store an opaque context reference in Work: `{ "service": "crm", "resource": "request", "id": "req_..." }`.
- CRM delegates scheduling and meetings to Work's `WorkEventResource` with participant lists.
- Users gain a unified "My Work" view across the entire 876 suite rather than checking siloed task lists in each individual app.

## Dynamic Provisioning & Materialized Configuration

CRM configuration (priorities, categories, subcategories, forms) is tenant-owned but platform-bootstrapped:

- The platform defines published defaults in the `application/876-crm` provisioning catalog.
- When an organization is provisioned onto CRM (`workspace.crm.ensure`), defaults are materialized into tenant-scoped database rows with a `provisioningKey`.
- Organizations can freely customize names, colors, icons, and sort orders in CRM Settings without risk of platform reconciliation overwriting their customizations.

## Caller Tiers

| Caller                                            | Entrypoint           | Credential                                           |
| ------------------------------------------------- | -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Console, administering any organization           | `@876/crm/operator`  | internal key, server-only                            |
| The standalone app, acting for a signed-in member | `@876/crm/service`   | internal key, server-only, org resolved from session |
| Pure wire contracts and schemas                   | `@876/crm/contracts` | types only, no runtime credential                    |
